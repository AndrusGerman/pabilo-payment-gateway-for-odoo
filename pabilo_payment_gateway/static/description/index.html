Verify pago móvil, bank transfers and Binance Pay without leaving the payment screen

Built for Venezuela. The customer pays and shows a reference number. Checking it by hand means opening the bank app, finding the transaction and coming back to the POS while the line waits. This module solves it inside Odoo: the cashier types the last 6 digits of the reference and Odoo confirms the payment against the bank in seconds.

How it works

  1. The customer pays by pago móvil, bank transfer or Binance Pay to your account.
  2. In the POS the cashier picks the Pabilo method, types the last 6 digits of the reference on a numeric keypad and confirms the date, which comes pre-filled with today.
  3. Odoo checks the bank through Pabilo and confirms the payment line. If anything is off, the cashier sees the exact reason — amount mismatch, reference already used, payment not found — instead of an endless wait.

What is included

One method, three rails
Pago móvil, bank transfers and Binance Pay with the same payment method.
Multiple bank accounts
If you have more than one account, the cashier picks which one received the payment.
No duplicate charges
A reference that was already used is rejected, so the same payment cannot settle two sales.
Audit trail
The reference and the Pabilo payment ID are stored on the POS payment and printed on the receipt.
Clear error messages
“Amount does not match”, “payment not found”, “reference already used”: the cashier sees the real reason, not a generic error.
Synced bank accounts
Your Pabilo accounts are synced into Odoo in read-only mode.
Beyond the POS
Manual verification and payment links from payment transactions.
Multi-currency
VEF, USD and EUR.

Setup

  1. Install the module.
  2. In the Pabilo settings, paste your API Key (appKey) and press Sync Accounts.
  3. From the Pabilo menu, create the payment method, choose the bank account and save.
  4. Add the payment method to your point of sale. Done.
A Pabilo account is required.
This module is the connector to the Pabilo payment-verification service (pabilo.app), where your bank accounts and credentials are managed. Your bank credentials are never stored in Odoo.

Compatibility

Odoo 16.0 and 17.0, Community and Enterprise editions. Requires the Point of Sale, Accounting and Payment apps.

Banks supported through Pabilo: Banco de Venezuela, Provincial, Mercantil, Banesco, Banco Plaza, Binance Pay and notification-based accounts.
